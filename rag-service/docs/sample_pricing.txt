Pricing and Payment Information

Our pricing is competitive and transparent. All prices listed on our website are in US dollars and do not include applicable taxes. Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your shipping address and local tax rates. The final price including all taxes and fees will be displayed before you confirm your order.

We offer several payment methods for customer convenience. Accepted payment methods include major credit cards such as Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. We also accept debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. For business customers, we offer purchase orders and net-30 payment terms subject to credit approval.

Financing options are available for purchases over $200. Customers can choose installment plans of 3, 6, or 12 months through our financing partner. Interest-free financing is available for 3-month and 6-month plans. The 12-month plan carries a low fixed interest rate. Approval for financing is subject to a credit check and is not guaranteed.

We run seasonal promotions throughout the year. Major sales events include Black Friday, Cyber Monday, and our anniversary sale in June. During these events, discounts can range from 10% to 50% off regular prices. Newsletter subscribers receive early access to sales and exclusive discount codes.

For bulk orders and corporate purchases, volume discounts are available. Orders of 10 or more units of the same product qualify for a bulk discount of 5% to 15% depending on quantity. Please contact our corporate sales team for a customized quote for large orders.

Price matching is available within 14 days of purchase. If you find a lower price from an authorized retailer for the same item, we will refund the difference. The price match request must be submitted with proof of the lower price, including the retailer name, product link, and advertised price.
