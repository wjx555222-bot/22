Refund and Return Policy

Our refund policy allows customers to return most products within 30 days of the purchase date for a full refund. To be eligible for a return, items must be in their original condition, unused, and in the original packaging. All accessories, manuals, and included items must be returned with the product.

For electronic devices such as smartphones and laptops, the device must not have been activated or registered with a personal account. If the device has been activated, a restocking fee of 15% will be applied to the refund amount. This policy ensures that returned devices can be properly reset and resold.

To initiate a return, customers must contact our support team to obtain a Return Merchandise Authorization number. Returns without an RMA number will not be accepted. Once the RMA is issued, customers have 14 days to ship the item back to our returns center.

Refunds are processed within 5 to 10 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. The refund will be issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. For credit card purchases, it may take an additional 3 to 5 business days for the refund to appear on your statement, depending on your bank's processing time.

Certain items are not eligible for return, including opened software, downloadable products, gift cards, and personal care items. Additionally, clearance items and final sale items cannot be returned or exchanged. These exceptions are clearly marked on the product page and during checkout.

For defective products, we offer a replacement or full refund regardless of the return window. If you receive a defective item, please contact us immediately with photos of the defect. We will provide a prepaid return shipping label for defective item returns.
